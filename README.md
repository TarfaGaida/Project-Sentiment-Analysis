📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer reviews and ratings to understand customer satisfaction, sentiment, and key factors influencing product performance.

The analysis combines customer ratings and review text to identify patterns in customer feedback and generate actionable insights for improving product quality, customer experience, and customer retention.

🎯 Business Objectives
Understand overall customer satisfaction based on product ratings.
Analyze customer sentiment from review text.
Identify common issues mentioned in low-rated reviews.
Identify characteristics of highly rated products.
Translate customer feedback into actionable business recommendations.


📊 Key Questions
What is the overall distribution of customer ratings?
How does customer sentiment vary across different rating levels?
What issues are commonly mentioned in negative reviews?
What characteristics are associated with highly rated products?
What actions can be taken to improve customer satisfaction?


🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Python
Pandas & NumPy – Data Cleaning & Manipulation
Matplotlib & Seaborn – Data Visualization
NLP / Sentiment Analysis – Customer Review Analysis
Jupyter Notebook – Exploratory Data Analysis


🔍 Analysis Process
1. Data Cleaning
Handling missing values
Removing duplicate records
Cleaning and preprocessing review text
Preparing rating and review data for analysis
2. Exploratory Data Analysis
Rating distribution
Product performance analysis
Relationship between ratings and sentiment
Identification of patterns in customer feedback
3. Sentiment Analysis

Customer reviews were analyzed to identify positive and negative sentiment and understand how sentiment relates to customer ratings.

4. Customer Feedback Analysis

Negative and positive reviews were examined to identify recurring topics, product issues, and attributes contributing to customer satisfaction.



💡 Key Insights
Low-rated reviews highlight issues related to product quality and usability that require further investigation.
Highly rated products provide examples of product attributes associated with positive customer sentiment.
Customer feedback can be used to identify specific areas for product and customer experience improvement.


🚀 Business Recommendations
Improve Low-Rated Products
Investigate recurring complaints, particularly regarding product quality and usability.
Replicate Top-Performing Products
Identify successful attributes from highly rated products and apply them to lower-performing products.
Strengthen Customer Loyalty
Provide rewards, discounts, and exclusive offers to loyal customers while addressing recurring complaints.


📈 Potential Business Impact
↑ Customer Satisfaction
↑ Product Ratings & Sentiment
↑ Customer Retention
Better Product Decisions
